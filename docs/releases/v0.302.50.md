# Codegraff v0.302.50

## Continue conversations in new directions

- `/fork` and ACP `session/fork` create an independent conversation while leaving the original available to resume.
- Subagents and workflows can explicitly fork history from the current conversation, a project-local saved session, or a retained worker.
- Retained workers can continue their own history or become the source of independent fan-out. Ordinary delegation stays lightweight; retaining a worker remains opt-in.

## Keep workflows moving toward a report

- Synthesis has a bounded evidence-gathering phase followed by a tool-free reporting request, rather than investigating indefinitely.
- Cancelled or exhausted synthesis workers are not automatically restarted.
- If the final stage fails, earlier findings remain available and are clearly marked incomplete.
- Repeated-tool guards survive worker retries and catch equivalent repeated reads.

## Reliability

This release also incorporates the current main-branch fixes for cancellation, peer-message delivery, platform cleanup, and regression-test stability.
