2. **How to Agree on GitHub.** To agree to this Agreement, post a comment on
   your pull request containing the following exact sentence:

   ```text
   I have read the Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and I hereby irrevocably agree to its terms.
   ```

   An automated check verifies that the comment comes from the pull request
   author, records your GitHub user ID, username, pull request number,
   timestamp, and the SHA-256 digest of this `CLA.md` on the repository's
   `cla-signatures` branch, and marks the `CLA` check as passing for your
   current and future pull requests.

   a company or other legal entity, ensure you have authority to bind that
   entity under this Agreement, or contact
   [rach@standardharness.com](mailto:rach@standardharness.com) to execute a
   separate written corporate CLA.
